(justify your choice against: requirement stability, risk/safety, timeline, and customer involvement)

project 1: Waterfall
- requirement stability:  Requirements are unusually well understood and stable
- risk/safety: the failure mode and required warning behaviour are precisely specified by engineering analysis before any coding begins,
  A defect in this software could endanger lives, so the cost of a field-discovered defect is extremely high
- timeline: there is no competitive pressure to release quickly, certification timelines- 12 to 18 months, dominate the schedule

risk: If a stage is incomplete or there is an error in a previous stage, it could endanger lives of people

project 2: Incremental
- requirement stability:  simple marketing website: a home page, a photo gallery, an opening-hours and location page, and a contact form.
  although the owner openly admits she is 'not sure exactly what it should look like',
- timeline: Budget is small and the owner wants something live within three weeks in time for a local food festival.
  
risk: there are no safety risks or integration, and documentation is minimal, the design however will be difficult as the
owner tends to change her mind about colors for the site

project 3: Prototyping
- requirement stability:  connecting university clubs and societies with students looking to join activities, browse clubs, RSVP to events, in-app chat
- risk/safety: basic data protection practice around user accounts is expected. 
- customer involvement:  adapt based on user behaviour and feedback
- timeline: they specifically want to release something usable within a month and adapt based on user behaviour and feedback,
  rather than fully specifying the app upfront,
  
risk: prototyping can be time consuming and costly, there is not much time,
